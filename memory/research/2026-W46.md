## body: format notes
- **Length (this sample, n=60).** In the data, the ratio is highest for sub-15 s loops and for 61-120 s explainers. The 16-30 s bucket comes almost entirely from big channels (median 285k subs), so its ratio does not compare with the others.

  | Length | Videos | Median views | Median ratio | Median subs |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | ≤15 s | 16 | 42,635 | 39x | 183 |
  | 16-30 s | 16 | 35,841 | 0.25x | 285,500 |
  | 31-60 s | 13 | 60,208 | 4.9x | 41,200 |
  | 61-120 s | 6 | 75,259 | 42x | 773 |
  | >120 s | 9 | 88,772 | 6.3x | 12,200 |

- **The ≤15 s outliers have very low engagement.** Like rates are about 0.3% and there are 0-2 comments per 500k+ views, against 1.7-4.8% likes on the 30 s to 2:51 explainers. Because replays count as views, short loops inflate view counts. Do not copy the 11 s format on that evidence alone.
- **External length guidance (third-party, weak):**
  - OverseerOS, 16,537 videos: 16-30 s had the highest median views overall, but within-channel comparisons did not show it consistently beats longer Shorts, and retention was not measured ([link](https://www.overseeros.com/blog/best-youtube-shorts-length)).
  - A vendor guide suggests 25-60 s and says videos over 90 s underperform, with no independent data ([AITuber, May 2026](https://aituber.app/blog/what-happens-to-your-body-video-format/)).
  - Our PLAYBOOK target of 30-45 s is not contradicted by anything solid.
- **Pacing (vendor-described structure for this exact format, unverified):** scenario question by 0:03, then escalation in compressed time steps (hour, day, week, month), with a new organ added at each step, then the payoff and a one-line close ([AITuber](https://aituber.app/blog/what-happens-to-your-body-video-format/)). This matches our open-loop and payoff rule.
- **On-screen text and visuals:** I could not open any of the outlier videos (YouTube pages returned only the site chrome), so captions and visuals are **unverified**. Titles point to inside or x-ray views ("3D", "🩻", "Inside Your Body"), which matches our X-ray cyan style.

- **Thumbnails:** custom Shorts thumbnails rolled out from 2026-07-25, YPP channels first, desktop upload only, and A/B testing does not cover Shorts. Keep key content away from the edges because some surfaces crop near 3:2 ([ppc.land](https://ppc.land/youtube-ends-2-year-wait-for-shorts-thumbnails-but-blocks-a-b-testing/)).

## body: what creators report getting penalized for
Direct creator reports from Reddit were unavailable this week (403). The items below come from policy coverage and are labeled by source quality.
- **Generic or repetitive content (YouTube policy wording, clarified 2026-07-13).** Content that "looks like it's made with a template", has minimal variation across videos, or is "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates giving the impression of mass production" is not monetizable ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/07/13/youtube-inauthentic-content-monetization-policy-update/)).
  - Relevance to us: one recurring hero and style is fine, but script, structure and visuals must vary between episodes.
  - This week's "How Your Body Digests Food: A 3D Animation" clones are a live example of the pattern to avoid.
- **AI personas on sensitive topics, including health.** An AI presented as a human expert, such as an AI "doctor" giving wellness advice, is barred from monetization (same source). Our narrator must never claim to be a doctor or give personal medical advice. Point viewers to NHS/CDC-type sources instead.
- **Unsatisfying or shock content.** Repeated disturbing themes with no narrative, unrelated AI clips stitched together for shock, and realistic fake disaster footage (same source). This is relevant to the freefall and amoeba topics.
- **Shorts reach for unoriginal content (2026-10-01).** Shorts recommendations now favor original content. Channels that mainly re-upload others' videos get less reach. Monetization still follows the reused-content policy ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/10/02/youtube-wont-recommend-unoriginal-shorts/), [Android Headlines](https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/10/youtube-cuts-reach-reuploaded-shorts-originality-push.html)).
- **Upcoming Shorts revenue threshold (announced 2026-08-10, effective 2027-02-01).**
  - Shorts Creator Pool payouts will need 10M qualified Shorts views per rolling 90 days, checked monthly.
  - New Shorts YPP applicants will need 20M qualified views in 90 days plus 1,000 subs.
  - Reuploads, non-original content and bot views are excluded from "qualified" ([ppc.land](https://ppc.land/youtube-cuts-shorts-pay-for-channels-under-10m-views-from-february-2027/)).
- **Vendor-listed reasons for faceless demonetization (secondary, not YouTube wording)** ([OutlierKit, 2026-09-07](https://outlierkit.com/resources/faceless-youtube-channel-demonetized/)):
  - the same template with only the topic swapped
  - stock footage plus an AI voice with no original research
  - titles that promise what the video does not deliver
  - realistic AI visuals or voice without the synthetic-content label
- **Fact accuracy.** Guides warn that AI-written health scripts hallucinate facts ([source](https://www.revid.ai/blog/how-to-make-faceless-youtube-shorts)). Our sourcing rule already covers this.
