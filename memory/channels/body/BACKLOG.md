3. What happens when you get heartburn? (source hint: NHS "Heartburn and acid reflux" https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/heartburn-and-acid-reflux/, checked 2026-11-15: heartburn is "a burning feeling in the chest caused by stomach acid travelling up towards the throat (acid reflux)"; also a sour taste in your mouth; triggers include coffee, tomatoes, alcohol, chocolate, fatty or spicy food, being overweight, smoking, pregnancy, stress; self-help: smaller, more frequent meals, don't eat within 3 or 4 hours before bed, raise the head of the bed 10 to 20 cm (NHS says extra pillows are NOT advised); see a GP if it's most days or pharmacy medicines don't help.
