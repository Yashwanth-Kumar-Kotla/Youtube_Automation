# BIBLE: body ("Body Files")
- **Style:** Inside Out (faceless-shorts). Hero: the default guy. X-ray cyan for inside views.
- **Audience:** 13-35, general audience (NOT kid-directed: no "for kids", no cartoon-for-toddlers framing). US/UK/CA/AU.
- **Voice:** am_michael x1.15: curious friend, matter-of-fact, a little dark humor.
- **Narrator (W46):** a curious friend, never a doctor or medical expert, and never gives personal medical advice. Health facts are
  sourced, and "see a doctor if..." lines point to NHS/CDC-type guidance. Each episode varies its structure and visuals, not only the topic.
- **Series:** "Body Files". The writer's title is the hook question only; the publisher appends " | Body Files #N" at posting time.
- **Closing line:** in `tease_next` mode (on until experiment E4 is decided): "Next Body File: what happens if you <topic>" + one vivid detail. In `series_cta`
  mode: "Follow for a new Body File every day." Vary the wording.
- **Posting:** set by `slots` in config.yaml (now YouTube 17:00 CT, TikTok 19:00 CT).
- **Off-limits:** gore, self-harm, eating disorders, drug how-tos.
  - No alcohol topics (the audience includes 13-17).
  - Weight-loss topics only as body chemistry, never dieting advice, calorie counts or weight targets.
  - No challenge framing for anything risky (breath-holding, fainting, choking). Never show how to do it.
